About AIEA

Built for Singapore automobile importers and exporters.

The initial idea to form an association of automobile importers and exporters was first mooted when a group of car dealers sat down for their regular lunch get-together in late 1998 to exchange ideas and share industry news. At that time, rules and regulations pertaining to the parallel import of new cars were slowly being liberalised by the authorities and more players were entering the trade.

An association shaped by the trade

With the blessing of the National Environment Agency and the Land Transport Authority, the Automobile Importer & Exporter Association (Singapore) — AIEA — was officially registered with the Registrar of Societies on 11 January 2000 with ten founding members.

What AIEA Does

The objectives of the Association

AIEA represents Singapore automobile importers, exporters, parallel import dealers, and motor trade businesses through representation, authority liaison, trade standards, and member support.

About AIEA Browse members
01

Protect member interests

To encourage, promote and protect the interests of all its members.

02

Liaise with government bodies

To act as a liaison body and communication platform between the relevant government bodies and its members as a whole.

03

Upgrade the industry

To organise activities and programmes to improve the expertise and services of its members and upgrade the automobile trading industry as a whole.

04

Promote proper trade practices

To establish and promote just and proper trade practices amongst its members.

05

Connect members

To act as a medium of communications between its members.

06

Share industry data

To collect information, data and statistics relating to the industry for the benefit of its members.

Takata Airbag Recalls.

Members and the public whose parallel imported cars are affected by the Takata Airbag Recalls can consider engaging the services of AIEA-recommended workshops for rectification works. Cost of rectification works is strictly to be settled between the members/public and the respective workshop.
